Today, I began a free trial of Amazon Kindle Unlimited, and I’m determined to make the most of it. The first book on my reading list is The Art of Thinking Clearly by Rolf Dobelli—a title that has long intrigued me for its promise to sharpen cognitive judgment and decision-making.

As I’ve recently launched my personal website, I’ve committed to publishing one article each week. To stay consistent, I’m now working on a long-term content strategy.

There are countless topics I’m excited to write about—especially those that once puzzled me, but through trial and error, I’ve managed to resolve. For instance, how to configure a laptop into vertical clamshell mode, how to care for office plants in a low-maintenance yet effective way, and how to tinker with basic electronic components as a hobbyist.

These are the types of experiences I’m eager to share—not just for documentation, but in the hope they might help someone who’s struggling with the same questions I once had.
